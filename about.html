About Our Work

Open for Business in Greenfield!

Welcome to Z Alterations & Bridal Sewing LLC. With over 22 years of family experience, we provide expert tailoring, clothing alterations, and custom bridal sewing for every special event.

Located in Greenfield, Wisconsin, our shop is dedicated to making your clothes fit you just right. Whether you need your wedding dress fitted or quick fixes for everyday clothes, we handle every item with care and attention to detail.

Important Information
Satisfaction Policy

Not happy with our work? Please bring your garment back within 14 days with your yellow receipt, and we will fix it for free.

Please check that you like the fit and pins before we start working. After alterations are finished, any extra changes requested because of a change in mind or fit preference may cost extra.

Pickup Policy

Please pick up your clothes within 45 days. Clothes left longer than 45 days may be disposed of or donated.
