Our Tailoring & Sewing Services

From complex wedding dress alterations to daily wardrobe repairs, we offer over 30 years of family-owned precision craftsmanship.

Bridal & Wedding Gowns

Complete custom fitting, bodice reshaping, hem adjustments, bustle additions, corset backs, and delicate hand-beaded lace repair.

Prom & Formal Attire

Precision sizing for bridesmaid dresses, prom gowns, evening wear, and cocktail dresses for an elegant silhouette.

Suits & Everyday Tailoring

Sleeve shortening, jacket side adjustments, trouser tapering, hem lines (plain, Euro, jean), and waist alterations.
