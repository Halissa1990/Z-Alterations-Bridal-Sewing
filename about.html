About Our Craftsmanship

Featured on Greenfield: We're Open!

Welcome to Z Alterations & Bridal Sewing LLC. Built on a rich legacy of over 30 years of family-owned craftsmanship, we are proud to offer expert tailoring, precision alterations, and dedicated bridal wear design for every occasion.

Located in Greenfield, Wisconsin, our shop is dedicated to bringing your unique garment vision to life with passion, meticulous attention to detail, and dependable, timely service. Whether you need a delicate wedding gown fitted for your big day or custom alterations on everyday wardrobe pieces, we treat every garment with extreme care.

Why Choose Us?

30+ Years Experience

Bridal & Formal Specialists

From intricate lace details to multi-layer gown fitting, we specialize in high-end formal and wedding attire.

Local Community Focus

Customer Satisfaction Policy

We stand by the quality of our workmanship. Should you be dissatisfied, please return your garment with the original receipt within 14 days, and we will gladly perform alterations at no additional charge to ensure your satisfaction.
