Customer Satisfaction Policy

Not satisfied with our work? Please bring your garment back within 14 days with your yellow receipt, and we'll be happy to redo it at no extra charge.

Please make sure you are satisfied with the pinning and measurements before we begin alterations. Once alterations are completed, any changes or adjustments requested due to a change of preference or fit may be subject to an additional charge.
